Change Risk Scoring Engine

Auditable change risk scoring on 8 weighted dimensions, with inherent vs residual risk modeled from ISO 31000. Generates a CAB-ready narrative on an on-premise LLM. Designed for environments where regulators care.

Python 3.12 LangChain FAISS Ollama 131 tests ISO 31000
·· The Problem

30 to 60 minutes per change. Wildly inconsistent reviewers.

Every production change at a bank goes through a CAB. Analysts manually review each request, cross-reference past incidents, write risk assessments. Conservative reviewers block changes. Liberal ones miss critical risks.

  • ● Manual review per change
  • ● No standard scoring rubric
  • ● Mitigations not tracked across revisions
  • ● Cloud AI not allowed for sensitive change data
·· The Solution

8 dimensions, deterministic scoring, AI-narrated.

Scores each change on 8 weighted risk dimensions, retrieves similar past changes via vector search, generates a CAB-ready narrative on an on-premise LLM. Deterministic scoring stays auditable. The AI layer produces the explanation.

  • ● 8 weighted dimensions, 1-5 each
  • ● Inherent vs residual risk (ISO 31000)
  • ● RAG-retrieved similar past changes
  • ● All processing local, no data leaves the bank

·· The 8 dimensions

Each one is a question. Each one has a weight.

The deterministic scoring layer evaluates each change against eight dimensions weighted by configurable risk policy. The weighted average maps to a 1-5 risk band. Mitigations addressed in subsequent revisions reduce specific dimensions, producing a residual score that drives the Go / No-Go recommendation.

·· Designed for

Bank change advisory boards, regulated fintech release engineers, technology risk teams in PCI DSS, SOX, and FFIEC environments. Anyone who needs the auditability of deterministic scoring and the explanation of an AI narrative, without sending sensitive change data to a cloud model.

·· ABOUT

A delivery practitioner from one of the world's most regulated banks, returning with a portfolio of AI tools she built herself.

I built and delivered technology that runs regulated banking at HSBC, in Hong Kong and then London. From a J2EE onboarding platform serving a million members, to a global program managing $10B+ in institutional assets, to an Open Banking API platform connecting 2M+ customers across Europe. The thread across all of it: shipping change in environments where regulators, auditors, and customer trust are non-negotiable.

After relocating to San Jose, I took some time to settle in. I've since been upskilling on applied AI and shipping open-source tools for program problems I've seen at HSBC. Three are public on GitHub. More to come.

Looking for senior delivery and program roles where this combination is useful.

◆ Pre-kickoff · Risk surfacing 01

Pre-mortem question generator

Input a project plan. Output the top ten questions a smart pessimist would ask before kickoff, so the risks get surfaced while there's still time to mitigate them.

◆ Planning · Forecasting 02

Roadmap reality check

Compares stated commitments against velocity and dependency data, generates a confidence score for each milestone before the exec has to ask.

◆ Shipping · Automation 03

Launch readiness checklist generator

Input a feature description and target launch date. Output a tailored checklist covering legal, support, marketing, oncall, monitoring, and rollback, with owners and due dates already filled in.

◆ Mid-execution · Drift detection 04

Sprint goal drift detector

Every Friday reads the sprint board, compares to the sprint goal, flags tickets that don't ladder up so scope creep gets caught mid-sprint instead of mid-quarter.

◆ Post-incident · Quality scoring 05

Postmortem quality scorer

Reads an incident postmortem, scores it on completeness covering root cause, contributing factors, action items, and blameless tone, suggests what's missing.
